## Meet with Skye - Sep. 23

We met with Skye over Zoom at 3:15pm on Wednesday afternoon. During this meeting, myself, Aryan, and Martin each described one of the three main concepts to Skye according to the distribution found in the minutes from Sep. 20. Adewola took notes during the meeting. These notes are in the Minutes folder (`Minutes/Stakeholder Concept Review Notes.pdf`).

This meeting was very valuable for all of us in deciding on our final concept going forward.

One important piece of info we gained is that Skye has epilepsy, so having lights near the face that may flash ro change color rapidly are a no-go. This ruled out our idea of having glasses displaying direction with LEDs, unless we could find a way to make them transition softly enough so as to not trigger a seizure. We did not want to take this risk.

Skye was fond of the compass device, which functionally was similar to the glasses but took on a significantly different form factor. She liked that it features multiple indicators for sound sources (primary/secondary) so that she isn't limited to one direction of focus, in case she may want to listen to multiple people in a conference. However, she thinks it may be easy to forget or lose. She suggested an alternative form factor, such as a watch or other accessory, rather than a handheld object.

Skye found the idea behind the headband device to be alright, but the placement was likely incompatible with her use case. The band being on the head could be seen as unprofessional, as she is not able to wear nonreligious head coverings at work. She suggested that tactile feedback could work well for her if implemented on other areas of the body, such as the wrist (like a smart watch, which she already uses).

After the meeting was concluded, our team discussed the feedback and began converging on a final concept that integrated a couple of our original concepts together with Skye's feedback. Our general idea is a wristwatch form of the SONIC compass, with vibration implemented. This would allow for detailed direction tracking when looking at the watch, and immediate notification of important sounds if not looking at the watch.
